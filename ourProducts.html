MATERIALS & SOURCING

Materials That Matter

Birkenor works with responsibly sourced fabrics chosen for longevity, comfort, and environmental respect.

Our MicroModal is derived from sustainably managed European beechwood forests and is OEKO-TEX certified — free from harmful chemicals.

Our shirting fabrics are selected for durability, breathability, and refined texture — prioritizing mills that uphold ethical production standards

We choose fewer materials.
We choose them carefully.
